# Market Sentinel

Market Sentinel is a personal market intelligence platform designed
to monitor financial markets, company fundamentals, news and
cybersecurity trends in order to detect potentially significant
investment opportunities and risks.

## Objectives

- Monitor financial markets
- Collect company fundamentals
- Aggregate financial and cybersecurity news
- Detect significant events
- Calculate investment opportunity scores
- Generate alerts
- Track historical recommendations
- Backtest the strategy

## Architecture

Data Sources
    ↓
Collectors
    ↓
PostgreSQL
    ↓
Analysis Engine
    ↓
Scoring
    ↓
Alerts
    ↓
Dashboard
